# Bride Experiment Protocol

## Purpose

Every donor mechanism is a hypothesis. Frankenstein v0.1 is the frozen champion. A challenger enters production only when it fixes a preregistered failure or improves a preregistered longitudinal metric without weakening authority, replay, provenance, portability, boundedness, or operational cost.

## Evidence classes

Tier A is deterministic evidence reproduced inside Bride. Tier B is preserved donor evidence not yet reproduced in Bride. Tier C is design history or an unqualified hypothesis. Tier D is frozen-case live-model or human evaluation. Evidence tiers are never silently upgraded.

## Qualification loop

For each candidate, freeze the baseline scenario and metric before implementation. Implement the smallest donor mechanism behind a bounded interface. Run local deterministic tests. If red, diagnose the smallest cause, correct only the candidate, and rerun the same frozen test. If green, compare against the frozen Frankenstein baseline. A cognition candidate must improve at least one preregistered behavioral metric by 0.10 absolute or convert a frozen failure to complete success, without material regression elsewhere. Automatic promotion also requires complexity cost no greater than 0.35.

Infrastructure candidates are judged by reproducibility, provenance, attribution, and semantic noninterference rather than by pretending that test infrastructure makes the character more human-like.

## Promotion states

PROMOTE means eligible for integration into the v0.2 candidate core after full baseline regression. PROMOTE_INFRASTRUCTURE means eligible as evaluation or provenance machinery, not as a cognition claim. QUALIFY_ADAPTER means useful at a host boundary without becoming subject authority. HOLD means useful evidence exists but the mechanism has not earned production complexity. BLOCKED means a necessary external or live-model reproduction is absent. REJECT means the current Frankenstein baseline already supplies the benefit or the challenger failed its frozen gate.

## Required invariants

The host remains objective-world authority. The subject owns beliefs, relationships, commitments, expectations, goals, concerns, and admitted private experience. The canonical ledger owns history. Projections remain rebuildable. Retrieval changes accessibility, never truth. One decision authority selects actions. Renderers own wording only. Capabilities remain default-deny. Planner state cannot bypass action competition. Private thought cannot become a world fact by being generated. Perception cannot rewrite objective reality.

## Longitudinal evaluation

Promotion into Frankenstein v0.2 requires matched histories, restart continuity, renderer substitution, replay, recovery from failed plans, actor-specific social differentiation, prospective-memory probes, repetition and silence checks, and computational-cost measurements. Recurrent or plastic mechanisms additionally require frozen versus plastic ablation, reversal learning, checkpoint/restart equivalence, and catastrophic-interference probes.
