# Research Timeline

This timeline is a compact evidence index. The README contains the full narrative and interpretation.

**2023:** Earlier persona-continuity work and the Ratican/Hutson line established the recurring problem: a character should survive renderer/model changes rather than being recreated by a prompt.

**2025-04-22:** Omnicore / Project Persona documented the .snp portable-character idea, tiered persistent memory, cognitive-state monitoring, optional inner thought, local deployment tiers, and the proposed six-dimensional emotion model.

**2026, early and mid year:** PersonaConsole and persona_engine_PYTHONX expanded portability, deterministic state, memory, renderer separation, and character cartridges. Wayfarer formalized objective-world authority, continuity ledgers, renderer firewalls, portability, and semantic renderer-swap measurement. Ensemble explored noncanonical candidate ecology, initiative, typed appraisal and expression gating.

**2026-09-10:** A controlled DUCK donor qualification found that representations are insufficient when causal arbitration is wrong. TinyPersona-style spatial filtering and room/world dynamics were useful, but donor graph authority had to be restricted. A later audit found a production-host bypass and character convergence under generic control.

**2026-09-11:** FirstPersonLoop isolated the rule that raw numeric mechanics must be translated into first-person experience. It also exposed a crucial failure: unconstrained model output can reintroduce machine telemetry through thought or speech. This motivated provenance-based experiential authority. Persona Connectome work in the same period explored protected identity anchors and spreading activation.

**2026-09-15:** Digital Subject / Persona-and-Jelly-Sandwich emerged as a small persistent-organism champion with homeostasis, sensorium, unattended life, relationships, autobiography, expectations, commitments, and objective outcome separation. A durable research rule was established: renderer improvements do not count as cognitive improvements.

**2026-09-17 to 2026-09-19:** Bicentennial-Man, Kurzweil-Brain-Experiments, and Pretorius-Neural-Network shifted attention toward developmental divergence, matched histories, counterfactual environments, recurrent policy, and plasticity.

**2026-09-20:** npc-steering-plus tested activation-space affect steering. Its card-versus-steering ablation found only a small visible-distinctness gain over a strong character card but a larger state-tracking improvement, making it promising but not yet Bride-qualified.

**2026-09-22 to 2026-09-24:** Jelly-Psiduck connected private cognition back into subjective experience, memory, bounded appraisal, and later conduct. Frozen matched-history ablations were created so the inner-ear pathway could be lesioned rather than assumed useful.

**2026-09-24 to 2026-09-25:** Pretorius v0.3 work emphasized inherited-character continuity rather than manufacturing personality through simulated childhood trauma. Reconstructed history and lived post-instantiation experience were separated, and a hash-chained lived ledger became a central provenance mechanism.

**2026-09-26 to 2026-09-28:** Kiki-Mind strengthened the distinction between canonical history and disposable projections. Developmental evidence and the proposed Subjective Frame receipt made retrieval policy and renderer context attributable.

**2026-09-29:** The-Doctor-Lives integrated recurrent policy, salience, interoceptive lag, unresolved loops, replay and consolidation. Its later causal audit produced both positive and null results: recurrent policy and reinforcement were causal, while some spreading-activation and self-model probes were inert in the tested pathway. Concern accumulation also exposed a chronic-high-alert failure mode.

**2026-09-30 to 2026-10-04:** Kiki, Calibos, agent chassis work, game-night, and Frankenstein Village supplied operational persistence, separate-agent boundaries, autonomous scheduling, and world-host patterns.

**2026-10-05:** Anima was reviewed as a major lifecycle donor. Frankenstein v0.1 then consolidated the production continuity kernel around a sealed origin, canonical event ledger, rebuildable projections, typed state, subjective firewall, replaceable renderer/interpreter, single action authority, capability gate, bounded heartbeat, and additive memory consolidation.

**2026-10-06:** The Bride of Frankenstein begins. Frankenstein v0.1 is frozen as champion. Donor mechanisms are no longer accumulated by intuition. Each must beat a preregistered baseline or improve research reproducibility before it can enter the v0.2 candidate trunk.
