# ChatGPT-Recovered Research History

This file records prior research information recovered from project conversations where the corresponding experiment was not necessarily preserved completely on a current GitHub default branch.

Recovered material is not stronger evidence than executable repository artifacts. Its purpose is to preserve lineage, failed ideas, experiment rationale, and donor hypotheses so the project does not repeatedly rediscover or silently rewrite its own history.

Recovered threads include the Omnicore to Persona Engine to Wayfarer and DUCK lineage; the separate Pretorius and PersonaConsole C99 to V4, V5, and V6 line; Gelatinblob recurrent-plasticity work; the Persona-and-Jelly digital-organism pivot; DUCK compact mechanism experiments involving fatigue, affiliation, competence, relationship memory, affect residue, concerns, habits, partner reliability, prospective cues, subject-owned location facts, and delayed-credit traces; prior global ablations that rejected several redundant commitment, reliability, and belief unions; multi-character tests in which generic host control caused behavioral convergence; and the finding that renderer or model substitution must be treated as an explicit experimental variable rather than inferred from persistent files.

One recovered compact experiment lineage, EXP-002 through EXP-007, moved through fatigue, affiliation and competence, relationship memory, affect residue, concerns, habits, partner reliability, uncertainty policy, bounded concerns, prospective cues, subject-owned facts, and eligibility traces. Global ablation rejected several larger unions and helper mechanisms. Accepted simplifications tended to be smaller: derived concern views, trace-backed immediate credit, removal of dead idle-habit machinery, and reduced capacities.

Another recovered result found that multiple nominally distinct characters could converge on the same host-level response despite differing internal records. Bride treats this as evidence that stored individuality without causal policy influence is insufficient.

When recovered material becomes material to a promotion decision, Bride must locate the original executable artifact or reproduce the experiment under the current frozen protocol.
