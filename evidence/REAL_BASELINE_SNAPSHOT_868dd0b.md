## Cost observations

Cost is recorded as evidence, not used as a proxy for cognitive quality. DUCK, Jelly, TinyPersona, Doctor-Lives, and involuntary expression remain within the current qualified budget in all six jobs. Offscreen catch-up remains a hold because its measured latency increase is materially larger than the ordinary per-probe budget. Contextual plasticity is held by complexity even though its behavioral and replay gates pass.

Cross-platform wall-clock timings vary because SQLite and runner performance vary. Verdict consensus, quality scores, causal effects, and invariants are stable across the matrix.

## Scientific interpretation

The strongest result is not the number of promoted donors. It is that several plausible donors failed to beat the frozen champion. Pretorius recall and Digital Subject continuity influence are retained as explicit null/redundancy results rather than folded into the architecture anyway.

A `PROMOTE` result in this snapshot means “advance to production-bridge qualification.” It does not authorize copying the laboratory selector into `src/frankenstein/`. Production integration must preserve Frankenstein's single DecisionEngine, authority boundaries, deterministic replay, and ablation path. Only a separately green integration candidate may become the v0.2 default.
